Water treatment services

Water treatment for boilers and cooling towers.

Chemtec Products Company is an industrial water treatment company in Souderton, Pennsylvania. We supply the product, run the program and test your water on site, for open and closed systems in Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Delaware and Maryland.

Boiler water

Scale on the tubes and corrosion in the metal both cost fuel and shorten the life of a boiler. We descale, keep the boiler running efficiently, and run inhibitor programs for high pressure steam and low pressure boilers, open or closed, steam or hot water. Each boiler is tested on every visit.

What the program assumes on your side

  • Feedwater under 2 ppm total hardness.
  • Feedwater held above 180°F (high pressure) or 160 to 180°F (low pressure).
  • 70 to 80 percent condensate return.
  • Daily blowdown by your operator.
  • Boiler conductivity held to the manufacturer's spec: 2,000 to 2,500 (high pressure) or 3,000 to 4,000 (low pressure).

Checked every visit

  • Conductivity
  • Inhibitor level
  • Sulfite
  • Alkalinity (P, M and O)
  • Total hardness
  • Visual: clear, cloudy or tinted

Feedwater

The water going into the boiler decides how hard the boiler has to work. Hard or cold feedwater means more scale and more oxygen in the system. We test it at the same visit so the boiler readings make sense.

Checked every visit

  • Conductivity
  • Hardness
  • Temperature
  • Visual

Cooling towers

A cooling tower concentrates minerals as water evaporates, and scale builds where the water runs hot. We descale and treat the tower, keep the inhibitor and algaecide feed where they should be (we make the algaecide), and set bleed-off by conductivity. If a feed pump needs adjusting, we adjust it on the spot.

Checked every visit

  • Conductivity against the bleed-off setpoint
  • Inhibitor level
  • Hardness
  • pH
  • Alkalinity (P, M and O)
  • Visual

The field service visit and report

What the report measures, in plain words.

It starts with a free risk assessment of your system, no charge and no obligation. After that we do the testing for you. A Field Service Report is yours on request, on the schedule that fits your plan: each reading next to the range your program calls for, then a short summary of what we did and what we need from you.

Conductivity
How much dissolved material is in the water. It climbs as water evaporates, and it tells us when to blow down a boiler or bleed off a tower.
Inhibitor level
How much treatment is in the system. Too low and the metal is not protected. Too high and you are paying for product you do not need.
Sulfite
Takes dissolved oxygen out of boiler water. The right level means oxygen is not attacking the metal.
Alkalinity (P, M and O)
Three standard readings of the water's buffering capacity. They show whether scale or corrosion is more likely.
Hardness
Calcium and magnesium, the minerals that turn into scale on hot surfaces.
pH
How acidic or alkaline the water is. On a cooling tower program, for example, the range is 8 to 9.
Recommended range
The target for each reading, set by your type of boiler or tower. In range needs nothing. Out of range gets a fix and a note.
Summary and recommendations
What we adjusted during the visit, what we are watching, and anything your operators need to keep up between visits.
