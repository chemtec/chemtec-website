The founder

James “Dapper” McKeag.

Started Chemtec Products Company in March 1982 in the garage of his house. Chemical engineer, Air Force veteran, founder.

1982 to 2021. The company he started is still in his family and still in the warehouse he moved it into.

His story

From a garage in Souderton.

In Pittsburgh, James drove tankers for Texaco and hauled trash, and took a chemical engineering degree at the University of Pittsburgh. He then worked for the two biggest names in the field, Nalco and Chemtreat. Industrial water treatment was the focus of his whole working life.

In March 1982 he started Chemtec in the garage of his house. The company outgrew the garage and moved into the warehouse in Souderton it still runs from today.

He was a proud US Air Force veteran who served in Thule, Greenland. He passed on 22 October 2021.
