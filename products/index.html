Product areas

What we supply.

Boilers and cooling towers are the core. The rest are smaller lines we still carry.

Boiler treatment

Descaling, efficiency and inhibitor programs for high pressure steam and low pressure boilers.

Cooling tower treatment

Descaling, inhibitor and algaecide for cooling towers, checked against their ranges every visit. We make the algaecide, and it is part of the program, not an add on.

Iron remover

Takes iron stains off sidewalks.

Irrigation rust preventer

A liquid fertilizer for sprinkler systems and irrigation lines that keeps the water from leaving rust behind. A smaller line we still supply. Ask about it on the call.

Windshield washer fluid

Two grades, zero degree and minus 20, for fleets, farms and anyone keeping vehicles on the road through the winter. Ask about it on the call.
