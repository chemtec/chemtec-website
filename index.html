The field service visit

Every visit, four steps.

It starts with a free risk assessment of your system, no charge and no obligation. Then the same routine on every visit, so nothing gets missed and you always know what we did.

Step 1
01
Test

Every system, on site.

Boiler, feedwater and cooling towers. Conductivity, inhibitor, sulfite, alkalinity, hardness and pH, each reading checked against the range your program calls for.

Step 2
02
Adjust

Fixed while we are there.

Chemical feed and pumps set on the spot, product topped up. Anything out of line is corrected before we leave, not written down for later.

Step 3
03
Report

Written up on request.

A Field Service Report on your schedule, at your request: every reading, what we changed today and what we need from your operators. Signed by the person who did the testing.

Step 4
04
Return

Back on schedule.

We come back on the cadence your system needs, and we are a phone call away in between. Reports will also sit in the customer portal once it opens.

The story

Family run since 1982.

It started in March 1982 in the garage of James “Dapper” McKeag’s house: a chemical engineer, an Air Force veteran, and years with Nalco and Chemtreat before he went out on his own. James passed in 2021, and everything he built the company on stayed: the work done properly, the testing done yourself, and the customer looked after first. Today it is run by Fay Dolan, owner and president, and her son Kyle Renz, vice president and James’s grandson. Same warehouse, same standard, the way he set it up.
