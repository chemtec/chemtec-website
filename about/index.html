About Chemtec

Family run since 1982.

It started in a garage in March 1982. The founder’s family runs it today from Souderton, Pennsylvania.

Veteran owned. Industrial water treatment sales and service.

Meet the founder, in the family’s words

James “Dapper” McKeag.

James “Dapper” McKeag created Chemtec in March 1982 having previously worked for Industry giants, Nalco and Chemtreat. Industrial water treatment has always been the focus of his work. Before this, he worked at Texaco oil, driving tankers and hauling trash in Pittsburgh. A graduate of The University of Pittsburgh having a degree in chemical engineering, he started out in the garage of his house and expanded into a warehouse where we remain today. James was a proud US Airforce veteran who served in Thule, Greenland. He passed October 22nd 2021.

“Never afraid of hard work, James always had a vision of how things should be done. I was always amazed at how he could do calculus in his head, math being a subject that he was passionate about. Being creative, on the other hand, was always a challenge that never failed to disappoint and constantly amused the people around him. He was always the first to have a smart remark, witty and funny. He had an absolute love for animals and anybody that was suffering. His heart was the biggest that I have ever come across and he will always have a place in mine.”

Then and now

Where it stands today.

  1. March 1982

    James starts Chemtec Products Company in the garage of his house. It outgrows the garage and moves into the warehouse in Souderton it still runs from.

  2. 2021

    James passes on 22 October. The company stays in the family, and so does everything he built it on: the work done properly, the testing done yourself, and the customer looked after first.

  3. Today

    Run by Fay Dolan, owner and president, and her son Kyle Renz, vice president and James’s grandson. Same warehouse, same standard, the way he set it up.
