How it works

  1. Tell us what you are paying

    Your company name, your account number if it is on the invoice, the invoice numbers and the amount.

  2. Finish on Stripe's secure page

    Sign in to your bank, or enter the routing and account numbers. Chemtec never sees your bank login.

  3. Your receipt follows

    Bank payments take up to four business days to clear. Stripe emails your receipt, and we apply the payment to your invoices.
